Tools we recommend

A short list of tools that come up again and again when members are getting set up, plus the free official sites every Australian business should know.

Free official resources (no affiliate links)

These are government websites. They're free, and they're the place to check before you pay anyone for a registration or "renewal". The Scam Watch guide (free when you sign up) covers the lookalike scams in detail. There is also a short public summary, and step-by-step guides for the ABN, business names, GST and grants.

Free, official, no affiliate link

Australian Business Register: apply for an ABN

Apply for an ABN for free, straight from the government. There's no fee and no renewal, so ignore anyone charging to "renew" it.

Go to abr.gov.au →
