About Australian Business Startup

A free website and newsletter for people starting and running a small business in Australia, and the Facebook group the site grew out of.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Home / About

Who runs this

Australian Business Startup is run by Enviga Pty Ltd (ABN 43 115 695 568). The same company publishes the website, sends the newsletter, and operates the Facebook group.

The guides are general information. They are not legal, financial or tax advice, and they are not a verdict on your own business. Where a page states a fee or a threshold, it links the government page it came from.

The Facebook group

The Facebook group started in December 2015. The site states a membership of 80,000+. It is a place for Australian founders and small business owners to ask practical questions of people who have already been there.

A lot of business material online is written for the United States, or is a sales pitch. This group is about Australian rules and Australian context.

What the website is for

The website publishes short guides for the steps people ask about when they start in Australia, and a public summary of the scams aimed at new businesses. The longer Scam Watch guide is emailed when you join the newsletter. It is not published as a search result.
