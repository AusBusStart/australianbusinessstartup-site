Common questions

How much does it cost?

Are ads labelled?

Yes. Every paid post is clearly labelled "Sponsored". A paid job post is also labelled as a paid job post.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Reach, reactions and comments vary from post to post. The numbers on this page are examples from single posts, not a promise of what your post will do.

We email you a payment link once we have agreed a date. Everything is handled by email. We don't take calls.

Can I advertise anything?

No. We don't accept scams, get-rich-quick offers or misleading claims, and we can decline any ad.
