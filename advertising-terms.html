You then accept the terms in force at the address above.

3. Products and bookings.

Inclusions are as confirmed in your quote, invoice or booking confirmation, or in our current price list. In summary:

(a) Sponsored post — one approved, labelled Facebook group post. It stays in the feed. We send a reach report about a week later. That week is a reporting window, not a deletion date. We may remove it if the offer is no longer current or it breaches these terms.

(b) Pinned post — a sponsored post pinned for 7 days, subject to availability, with the start date confirmed by us, then it stays in the feed on the same basis.

(c) Paid job post — one job advertisement posted by us, labelled as a paid job post and as Sponsored, up for at least 30 days (not pinned), or earlier if the role is filled. Australian businesses with an ABN only; real roles showing a pay rate or range. No commission-only roles, MLM, or ads that ask applicants to pay. Refundable if declined before posting.

(d) Member Spotlight — a short Q&A we write from your answers (you approve it first). One link and one image, labelled as sponsored, pinned for 7 days, subject to availability, with the start date confirmed by us, then it stays in the feed. Australian businesses with an ABN only.

(e) Sponsored website article — 1,000+ words on our website, labelled as sponsored, with up to two links tagged rel="sponsored" (no dofollow links; no insertions into existing articles). Original, useful to Australian small business owners and founders, approved by us first (we may ask for edits), live within 5 business days of approval and payment. Links must go to legitimate business sites. The end client must be disclosed. Not accepted: gambling, crypto, payday or short-term loans, or adult or misleading content. The multi-article rate applies only to sponsored website articles booked under this clause 3(e) by email and invoiced together, not to articles paid through the PayPal button and not to Boosted sponsored website articles under clause 3(e1). A month in which three or more of these articles are ordered and paid on one invoice is charged at the multi-article rate in your quote or current price list (for example, $192.50 inc GST each); otherwise each article is charged at the single-article price (for example, $220 inc GST).

(e1) Boosted sponsored website article — everything in a sponsored website article under clause 3(e), plus: (i) one sponsored Facebook group post linking to the article, starting with "Sponsored: [client name]", with key takeaways and a read-more link (not pinned, no @everyone tag); (ii) one mention in our next scheduled email newsletter, labelled "Sponsored by [client name]" next to the mention; and (iii) a reach and engagement report for the group post about 7 days after it is published. Each extra runs once. We schedule the group post and newsletter mention to fit our limits on sponsored group posts, so the dates are set by us. Boosted sponsored website articles are not counted toward, and do not receive, the multi-article rate in clause 3(e). The price is in your quote or our current price list (for example, $330 inc GST).

(f) Monthly Bronze, Silver or Gold sponsorship — inclusions are as set out in your quote or current price list. If you book Gold, your quote or confirmation will name one narrow line of business (for example, "accounting and bookkeeping"), decided by us acting reasonably at booking. During the term we will not sell paid placements to a competitor in that category. This does not restrict members' free posts, Promo Friday, the Introduce Yourself thread or comments. Posts are labelled as sponsored, we send a monthly reach report, and posts go live after we approve the copy and payment has cleared. Benefits run only during a paid period.

We may decline or postpone a booking if it does not meet these terms or we lack capacity. Preferred dates are a request, not a guarantee. Book a single group post via our website form, email or Messenger. Book a single sponsored website article or Boosted sponsored website article by paying through the PayPal button on our website, or by email to admin@australianbusinessstartup.com.au for an invoice. Book monthly sponsorships, and orders for the multi-article rate, by email to admin@australianbusinessstartup.com.au. We may request changes before publishing. Payment is due before we publish (or before a monthly period starts), unless your quote says otherwise. For a Member Spotlight, you pay after approving the draft and before we publish. If you pay for an article before we have approved it, send us your draft (or your brief, if we are writing it) by email after paying. We review it after payment and may ask for edits. If we decline it, or we have not received a draft we can approve within 60 days of payment, we refund in full what you paid for that article. You can also cancel for a full refund at any time before it is published (see clause 6). After a PayPal payment we will email you a tax invoice. We accept PayPal, invoice or bank transfer, as specified for the product. Paying does not mean the content will go live. If we cannot or will not run it, we refund that booking (see clause 6).

We do not accept scams, get-rich-quick offers, misleading claims, gambling, crypto, payday or short-term loans, or adult content, and we may refuse content that we reasonably consider unsuitable for our audience or that does not meet the rules for the product. Subject to the 12-month live period for website articles in clause 6, we may refuse a booking, request edits, or remove or unpin published content if it breaches these terms, is no longer current, must be removed by law or by a platform, or is the subject of a genuine complaint we reasonably consider should be acted on. All paid content will be clearly labelled as sponsored or advertising in the text, in line with the AANA Code of Ethics and ACCC guidance on advertising and influencer marketing. Paid content is labelled in each channel where it appears: on the website, as sponsored; in a Facebook group post, at the start of the post (for example, "Sponsored: [client name]"); and in our newsletter, next to the item (for example, "Sponsored by [client name]"). Where Meta's branded content or paid partnership tools apply to a Facebook group post, we may use them. You must not ask us to hide that the content is paid advertising.

After the periods in clause 3, group posts stay in the feed unless removed under these terms. A sponsored website article is guaranteed to remain live for at least 12 months from publication, except if we remove it because it breaches these terms or you ask us to take it down. After that, it may remain at our discretion and may be removed if it is no longer current or if the site is restructured. Facebook (Meta) and other platforms are outside our control. If a platform removes, limits or delays your content, or is unavailable, that is not a breach by us. We will tell you if we become aware of a removal and will discuss a reasonable alternative or refund if we have not delivered the booking. We are not required to refund for a platform issue alone, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires otherwise. If you cancel a booking before we have published the content, we refund in full what you paid for that unpublished booking, subject to clause 7. Once content has been published, we do not refund, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it, or where we remove the content for our own reasons rather than because you breached these terms. In that case we refund a fair portion of the price, reflecting what was not delivered.

7. Monthly sponsorships and article bundles.

A monthly sponsorship has a minimum term of 3 months, then continues month-to-month. We invoice monthly in advance and issue a tax invoice each month. If your quote states a trial offer, that trial overrides the 3-month minimum. After the minimum term (or after a trial, if that applies), either party may cancel by giving 30 days' written notice. The sponsorship then ends at the end of the billing month in which that notice period expires. There are no part-month refunds. We may change the price of a monthly sponsorship by giving you 30 days' written notice. If we increase the price, you may cancel by written notice before the new price takes effect, and we will not charge you the increased price. The multi-article rate applies only to sponsored website articles under clause 3(e) that are booked by email and invoiced together (not articles paid through the PayPal button or Boosted sponsored website articles), and only in a month in which three or more of those articles are ordered and paid, at the rate in your quote or current price list. A month with fewer than three is charged at the single-article price. If a prepaid order of three or more articles is cancelled part-way and drops below three, articles already published are re-priced at the single-article price and the difference is deducted from the refund of unpublished articles. You will not be charged more than you have already paid.
