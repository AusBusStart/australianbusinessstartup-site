Episode 1: Solo founders, AI rules and getting paid

14 November 2026 · 10 minutes 25 seconds

This first episode talks through recent stories for Australian founders, including a US survey on small businesses and AI tools, research on solo founders and earnings, Canberra's draft voluntary AI guidelines and planned safety standards, what small suppliers have told the ABC about being paid around the David Jones administration, a way to think about Black Friday discounts (27 November 2026), the Productivity Commission's work on making it easier to start a business (submissions close 11 December 2026), a regional crowdfunding raise, and two current scams: fake online shopfronts, and calls pretending to be from Google. Meet the hosts

Bootstrap Bec and Venture Vinny are AI-generated cartoon characters, not real people. They are a way to talk through both sides of a story. Every episode says so on screen.

Bootstrap Bec

A Queensland bootstrapper who runs a coffee van.

"Show me the first sale"

Venture Vinny

A Sydney seed-funded tech founder.

"But does it scale?"

Each week they take opposite views of the news, then agree on one thing an Australian founder can actually do.

What to expect

A new episode each week, usually about ten minutes long. The show looks at Australian startup and small-business news, and at global stories that are heading our way. Sources are listed in each YouTube description.
