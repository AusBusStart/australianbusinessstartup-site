How these fit together

Most people need an ABN if they are really in business. They need a business name only if they trade under something other than their own name. GST comes later for a lot of small businesses, and sooner for some. Grants are not a starting fund you can count on.

The step-by-step overview puts those in order. The structure guide is the decision that sits underneath the forms.

The Scam Watch summary is the short public version of the scams that show up once you register. The full guide, with the checklist, is what we email when you sign up.

For the official walk-through, use business.gov.au's guide to starting a business. For tools and the free government sites in one list, see tools we recommend.

Common questions

Are these guides personal advice?

No. They are general information for Australia, with links to the official source. They are not legal, financial or tax advice.

Where do the fees and thresholds come from?

Each figure is one an official page published, and the guide links that page. The pages were checked in October 2026. Fees are indexed and grant rounds close, so the government site is the current number.
