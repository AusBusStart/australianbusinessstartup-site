Tax

GST registration basics

GST is a 10% tax on most goods and services sold in Australia. Not every new business has to register on day one. Some do, even with almost no turnover. The rules below are the ATO's, checked in October 2026, not a tax opinion on your business.

1What is GST?

The ATO's how GST works page, updated 14 September 2026, describes goods and services tax as a broad-based tax of 10% on most goods, services and other items sold or consumed in Australia. Some sales are GST-free. Which ones are GST-free is specific, so use the ATO's pages rather than a shorthand.

If you are registered, you generally include GST in the price of taxable sales, and you claim credits for the GST in the price of things you buy for the business. You then lodge a business activity statement and pay the net amount, or receive a refund. You need an ABN before you can register.

2When do I have to register for GST?

Register when GST turnover is $75,000 or more, or you expect a new business to reach that in the first year. A non-profit organisation uses $150,000. Taxi, limousine and ride-sourcing travel must register regardless of turnover, and the ATO lists further cases below, including fuel tax credits.

The ATO's registering for GST page, also updated 14 September 2026, says you must register:

  • when your GST turnover is $75,000 or more
  • when you start a new business and expect turnover to reach that threshold in the first year
  • if a non-profit organisation has GST turnover of $150,000 or more
  • when you provide taxi or limousine travel for passengers, including ride-sourcing, regardless of turnover (owner-drivers and people who lease or rent a taxi)
  • if you want to claim fuel tax credits

The same page also lists income through the sharing economy or a digital platform, and gives food delivery as an example, with a pointer to the ATO's sharing-economy guidance. The more specific page, how GST applies when providing services (updated 20 August 2025), says you register when GST turnover is $75,000 or more, or when you want to claim fuel tax credits. Ride-sourcing is the activity the ATO calls out as registering regardless of turnover. Read both pages for the kind of platform work you do. Do not assume every app job forces a GST registration, and do not assume none of them do.

If none of those apply, registering is optional. If you choose to register, the ATO says you generally must stay registered for at least 12 months.

Once you are required to register, you have 21 days to do it. You only need to register once, even if you run more than one business. The ATO says penalties can apply if you do not register when you should. You may have to pay GST on sales from the date you were required to register, even if you did not include GST in those prices, plus penalties and interest.

3How is the $75,000 GST threshold worked out?

GST turnover is total business income, not profit. The ATO says you subtract GST included in sales to customers, and a list of other amounts (sales that are not connected with the enterprise, input-taxed sales, and sales not connected with Australia, among them). Use the ATO's worked examples rather than a back-of-the-envelope profit figure.

You reach the threshold if either:

  • current GST turnover (this month plus the previous 11 months) is $75,000 or more, or $150,000 or more for a non-profit organisation, or
  • projected GST turnover (this month plus the next 11 months) is likely to be at that level

When you project turnover, the ATO says not to include the sale of a business asset, or a sale you make only because you are ceasing the enterprise or permanently and substantially scaling it down. And even if current turnover is already over the line, you do not have to register if the ATO is satisfied your projected turnover will be under it.

If you are not registered, check each month. The 21 days starts when you exceed the threshold or when you are otherwise required to register, not at the end of the financial year.

4How do I register for GST?

You need an ABN first. The ATO says you can then register in Online services for business, by phoning its business enquiries line, or through a registered tax or BAS agent. You can also add GST in the same application as a new ABN on the Business Registration Service.

Once you have an ABN, the ATO says you can register:

  • through Online services for business
  • by phoning the ATO's business enquiries line (the number is published on the ATO page, not copied here in case it changes)
  • through a registered tax agent or BAS agent

You can also add GST in the same application as a new ABN on the Business Registration Service. The ATO confirms the registration in writing, including the date it takes effect.

You can ask to backdate a registration. The ATO says backdating is limited to four years, unless there is fraud or evasion, and the request is made by phoning the business enquiries line.

5What changes once I am registered for GST?

You include GST in the price of taxable sales, you can claim credits on GST in business purchases, and you lodge a business activity statement. The ATO tells you how often to lodge. On a GST-inclusive price, the GST is one-eleventh of the price, not one-tenth.

The ATO's how-GST-works page lists the ongoing jobs:

  • work out whether a sale is taxable, GST-free or input-taxed, and include GST in the price of taxable sales
  • issue tax invoices for taxable sales, and keep tax invoices for business purchases
  • claim GST credits for GST in the price of business purchases
  • account for GST on a cash or a non-cash basis (the ATO explains who can use which method)
  • set the GST aside so it is there when the activity statement is due
  • lodge activity statements, or an annual return if that is what the ATO has set for you, and pay what you owe or receive a refund

How often you lodge is part of the registration. Do not assume it is quarterly. Read the notice the ATO sends you.

Pricing is the practical change customers notice. A price that was $100 with no GST is not the same as a GST-inclusive price of $100. The ATO points to a GST calculator on MoneySmart if you want to strip GST out of a GST-inclusive amount. One tenth of the price is not the GST amount. GST of 10% means the tax is one-eleventh of a GST-inclusive price.

6What if I am not sure whether to register?

Registering too early means extra paperwork, and you generally stay in for 12 months. Registering late can mean GST you never collected, plus penalties. Ride-sourcing is the common case where people wait for $75,000 and were required to register from the first fare.

A registered BAS or tax agent can look at your actual turnover. The ATO pages linked above are the source to take to that conversation. Structure does not change the threshold: sole traders, partnerships and companies are all covered. See the structure comparison for how the tax return differs even when the GST test is the same.

Common questions

What is the GST rate in Australia?

The ATO describes GST as a tax of 10% on most goods, services and other items sold or consumed in Australia. Some sales are GST-free, and the ATO's pages are the list to use.

What is the GST registration threshold?

The ATO's registering-for-GST page, updated 14 September 2026, says $75,000 of GST turnover, or $150,000 for a non-profit organisation. Ride-sourcing and other taxi or limousine travel must register regardless of turnover.

Is GST turnover the same as profit?

No. GST turnover is total business income, not profit, with the subtractions the ATO lists. You look at the current 12 months and the projected next 12 months.
