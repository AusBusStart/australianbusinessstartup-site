About the community

Australian Business Startup began as a Facebook group in December 2015. It's a place where Australian founders and small business owners ask real questions and get straight answers from people who've been there.

A lot of business advice online is written for the US, or is a sales pitch in disguise. This community is about Australian rules, Australian context and members helping each other.

  • Practical beats hype.
  • Australian rules and context matter.
  • Founders help founders.
